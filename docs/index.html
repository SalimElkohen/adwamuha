Adwamuhā (أدومها) – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Adwamuhā is a free app for tracking daily good deeds and personal goals.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your goals, check-ins and settings are stored only on your device and are included in your device's own backup (for example iCloud Backup) under your control. We never receive them.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules two local reminders a day on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

Seasonal challenges

The app may download the list of seasonal challenges from a public file. This request contains no personal information.

Deleting your data

Use Settings › Erase all data in the app, or delete the app.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on this project's GitHub page.

سياسة الخصوصية – أدومها

لا نجمع أي بيانات. لا حساب ولا إحصاءات ولا إعلانات ولا تتبع. أهدافك وتسجيلاتك محفوظة على جهازك فقط وضمن نسختك الاحتياطية. التذكيرات محلية على جهازك. يمكنك مسح كل بياناتك من الإعدادات أو بحذف التطبيق.
